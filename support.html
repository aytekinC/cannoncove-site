Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag anywhere on the screen to aim the cannon; a dotted line shows where the shot goes. Release to fire. Clear every crate, barrel, stone and block of ice off the pier before your cannonballs run out.

I ran out of cannonballs. What now?

You can continue with 5 more balls for coins, or give up and try again. Each level is short, and a different angle often works better.

How do lives work?

You have up to 5 lives. A life is used when you give up or leave a level after firing; one life comes back every 30 minutes, or you can refill all of them with coins.

A purchase did not arrive.

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases at the bottom, with an internet connection. If the goods still don't appear, email us with the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Only purchases need a connection.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Progress is stored on your device only, so deleting the app resets it. Device backups (iCloud or computer) keep it.
