Effective date: October 20, 2026 · Applies to Cannon Cove for iPhone, version 1.0 and later until updated.

Cannon Cove ("the game") is a single-player puzzle game made by Aytekin Coşkun, an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens with information when you play.

In short: we do not collect any personal data. The game has no account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics service and no tracking. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

The game keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Game progress and settings: current level, coins, lives and their refill timer, boosters, sound / music / vibration settings, and which tutorial cards you have seen.
  • Purchase records: the App Store transaction identifiers of purchases already delivered, so that a purchase is never delivered twice.
  • A local game event log (levels started, won or lost, boosters used, purchases made). It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

We have no access to this information. Deleting the app deletes it. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, depending on your settings.

2. In-app purchases

Coin packs and bundles are optional and are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple gives the game a transaction identifier, which is stored on your device (section 1). Apple's handling of purchase data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising and tracking

This version of the game shows no advertising, contains no advertising SDK and does not track you across apps or websites. If a future version adds advertising, we will update this policy before that version is released and the App Store privacy information will be updated accordingly.

4. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal data, we collect none from children either. Purchases on a child's device follow the Family Sharing and "Ask to Buy" settings of the device.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK or US state laws) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete; you can remove everything the game stored by deleting the app. You can still contact us with any question or request.

6. Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the effective date.
