Effective date: October 20, 2026 · Applies to Cannon Cove for iPhone and Android.

Cannon Cove ("the game") is a single-player puzzle game made by Aytekin Coşkun, an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens with information when you play.

In short: we (the developer) do not collect personal data: there is no account, no sign-up and no analytics service, and your game progress stays on your device. The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob; the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described in section 3. On iPhone, ads are personalised with your device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when the game asks.

1. Information stored on your device

The game keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Game progress and settings: current level, coins, lives and their refill timer, boosters, sound / music / vibration settings, and which tutorial cards you have seen.
  • Purchase records: the App Store transaction identifiers or Google Play purchase tokens of purchases already delivered, so that a purchase is never delivered twice.
  • A local game event log (levels started, won or lost, boosters used, purchases made). It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

We have no access to this information. Deleting the app deletes it. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, depending on your settings.

2. In-app purchases

Coin packs and bundles are optional and are processed entirely by the store you installed the game from: Apple through the App Store (iPhone) or Google through Google Play (Android). We never see your name, Apple ID, Google account or payment details. The store gives the game a transaction identifier (on Android a purchase token), which is stored on your device (section 1). How the stores handle purchase data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) and tracking

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.): optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (extra coins, an extra life, extra cannonballs) and, from level 24, a full-screen ad between some levels. The one-time "No Ads" purchase removes the ads between levels.

To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes from your device:

  • a device identifier: the advertising identifier (Apple IDFA, only if you allow tracking; Android Advertising ID) or app- / developer-scoped identifiers,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (coarse) location can be estimated,
  • advertising data and product interactions (ads shown, taps, video views, rewarded ad completions),
  • diagnostics: crash logs and performance data (app launch time, hang rate, energy use).

Google processes this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

Your choices

  • iPhone (App Tracking Transparency): the game asks once, after you finish your first level, whether it may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not used and ads are not personalised with it; the game works exactly the same. You can change this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form asks for your consent at launch; without consent only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in the game under Settings > Privacy choices.
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information through the same consent tools.
  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are requested with a maximum content rating of "PG". Purchases on a child's device follow the Family Sharing and "Ask to Buy" settings of the device. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help to delete it.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK or US state laws) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data protection authority. We hold no personal data about you ourselves; game data is removed by deleting the app, and advertising data is handled by Google (you can reset your advertising identifier or use the choices above). Contact us with any question or request.

6. Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the effective date.
