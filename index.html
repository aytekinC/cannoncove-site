Knock every crate off the pier

Cannon Cove is a bite-sized physics puzzle game set in a sunny pirate harbor. Aim the cannon, fire, and topple stacks of crates, barrels, stones and ice from seesaws, tables, spinning platforms and rolling docks. 100 levels, powder kegs that chain-explode and four boosters.

Free to play, plays offline, no account needed.
